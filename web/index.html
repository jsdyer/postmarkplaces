Which office fits the date?

A name on an envelope can belong to different offices in different years. Search the spelling on the postmark and examine the dated entries.

Before Dartmouth had an office

Parks Canada dates the apparent beginning of postal service in Halifax to April 23, 1754. It records regular mail packets calling at Halifax after March 1788. These dates describe mail service across the harbour region. Dartmouth’s own office received official status in 1870.

Following the evidence

Each card follows one office through its recorded names. An open-ended entry means the source gives no later event. A year-only event may have occurred at any point in that year. For a new name or a gap, check Library and Archives Canada’s original post-office and postmaster records.
